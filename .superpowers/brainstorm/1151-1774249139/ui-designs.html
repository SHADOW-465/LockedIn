Design System
⬛
Background
#000000 — pure black. Same as the settings page. Cards sit above it as `bg-zinc-900` surfaces with `border-zinc-800` edges.
🔲
Cards
bg-zinc-900 (#18181b) · border-zinc-800 (#27272a) · rounded-xl/2xl. Exactly the settings card style. Rounded, floating, consistent.
📝
Text Hierarchy
text-white for titles · white/85 for body · white/50 for secondary · white/30 for labels. All legible. No invisible grey.
🔴
Red Accent
#ef4444 with a soft glow. Session indicator, active directive tag, master task border. Pulses subtly — alive, watching.
⭕
Rounded Everything
rounded-xl on cards, rounded-full on dots, rounded-2xl on the timer card. Soft, inviting, hypnotic — not harsh.
💫
Glows
Red dot glows. Timer ring has drop-shadow. Comply button is bright white. Light used sparingly — it draws the eye exactly where it needs to go.
