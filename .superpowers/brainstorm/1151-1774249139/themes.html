Why These Colours
Crimson
Blood. Pain. The colour of punishment and obedience enforced. Authority that is felt, not merely understood.
Amethyst
The hypnotist's colour. Psychological dominance — not force, but inevitability. You surrender because you can't imagine not surrendering.
Ice
Clinical sadism. Cold precision. The Dominant who watches without emotion. Your suffering is quantified, not savoured.
Gold
The gilded cage. Luxury submission — you are owned, but the ownership is exquisite. Golden chains are still chains.
Obsidian
Deep water submission. You sink willingly, slowly. Also the Care Mode colour — the only safe harbour in the void.
Bone
Stripped bare. Total exposure. White on black — nothing hidden, nothing left to protect. The purest submission.
