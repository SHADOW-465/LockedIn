# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Men practicing chastity and discipline, seeking a structured, gamified, and supportive environment to build better habits and self-control. They are in a context of self-improvement and potentially BDSM/chastity dynamics.

## Product Purpose
A digital companion and dashboard that tracks chastity time, logs behaviors (urges, touches, removals), enforces random check-ins, and provides a structured daily ritual (journaling, mood tracking, tasks) to maintain discipline. Success is measured by consistency and streak maintenance.

## Brand Personality
Disciplined, modern, premium, empowering. The tone is encouraging but firm ("Discipline is remembering what you want most").

## Anti-references
- Cluttered SaaS dashboards
- Generic gray/white corporate tools
- Overly explicit or cheap-looking adult sites
- Brutalist or overly aggressive designs (which the user explicitly rejected earlier)

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over clutter:** High information density but extremely scannable.
- **Premium Glassmorphism:** Depth, blurs, and layered surfaces to feel like a high-end operating system.
- **Electric Accents:** Monochromatic dark base with striking neon/lime accents to draw attention to critical metrics.
- **Rewarding Interactions:** Smooth, deliberate animations and feedback on every interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for primary text (white on dark zinc/black).
- Reduced motion support for animations.
- Clear touch targets for mobile/tablet scaling.
